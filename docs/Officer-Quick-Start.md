# Requesting training or travel — the whole process

1. Open **TPD_Travel_Request_Form.xlsm** (get it from the Training Unit or
   the shared drive). If Excel shows a yellow security bar, click
   **Enable Content** — the form's only automation is the submit button.

2. **Fill in the white boxes.** Hover any box for an example. Dates need
   real dates (08/24/2026), times need clock times (6:30 AM). Costs are
   your best available numbers — the Training Unit verifies everything.

3. Departure and return **times matter**: they decide which meals your per
   diem covers. Give your honest planned times.

4. Click **Submit to Training Unit** (or press **Ctrl+Shift+S**). An email
   opens, already addressed, with your form attached. **Look it over and
   press Send.** Nothing sends by itself.

That's it. You'll hear back with a notification sheet showing what's
prepaid, your per diem, and when your receipts are due after the trip.
**Keep every itemized receipt.**

Questions or a trip with unusual costs: call the Training Unit before
submitting.
